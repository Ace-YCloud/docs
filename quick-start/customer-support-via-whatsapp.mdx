WhatsApp gives customers a way to reach your business through the app they already use daily. This guide walks through setting up a full customer support workflow using YCloud's Inbox and automation features.

## Pre-preparation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a YCloud account">
    [Register](https://www.ycloud.com/console/#/entry/login?lastPath=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ycloud.com%2Fconsole%2F%23%2Fapp%2Fdashboard%2FgetStarted) your YCloud account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a WABA account">
    You need a Facebook account and a phone number capable of receiving voice calls or SMS, with no prior WhatsApp registration. See [Create a WhatsApp account](/quick-start/create-whatsapp-account) for full instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up customer support

### Step 1: Invite the customer service team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Users and Teams">
    Go to **Account (lower-left corner) > Settings > Users and teams > Invite user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign roles">
    Select **Service** as the role for customer support agents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Set customer assignment rules

Configure the invited agents into the WhatsApp phone number's assignment rules so that incoming customer inquiries are routed to the right team member promptly.

Go to **WhatsApp accounts > Number settings > Conversation assignment rules** to configure routing logic.

### Step 3: Configure automated messages

Automated messages handle common situations such as unassigned conversations, queued conversations, and timeout replies — improving response efficiency and customer experience.

Navigate to **WhatsApp accounts > Number settings > Automation** to set up:

- Welcome messages
- Queue notifications
- Timeout reply messages
- Out-of-hours responses

### Step 4: Configure a Chatbot

A Chatbot can automatically respond to customer inquiries, perform operations like tagging contacts or adding them to an unsubscribe list, and reduce the workload of your support team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Chatbot">
    Go to **Chatbot > Create Chatbot** and complete the basic configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Chatbot Flow">
    Set the flow trigger conditions, design the conversation flow, and activate the flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate the Chatbot with a phone number">
    Go to **WhatsApp accounts > Number settings > Chatbot**, add the Chatbot, and configure the reception schedule (working hours during which the bot handles incoming messages).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 5: Train your customer service team on Inbox

Inbox is YCloud's customer communication platform. Train your team on key features including:

- Shared inbox and conversation management
- Agent assignment and conversation routing
- Customer data and contact history
- Canned responses for common queries
- AI Copilot for suggested replies

### Step 6: Monitor performance

**Data analysis** — Use the Inbox analytics dashboard (**Inbox > Analytics**) to track team performance, response times, resolution rates, and conversation volume. This helps managers make data-driven decisions and optimize resource allocation.

**Conversation logs** — Access all historical conversations via **Inbox > Conversation logs**. Use the search and filter tools to locate specific conversations for quality review or spot checks.

## Best practice: design chat access points

Making it easy for customers to start a WhatsApp conversation increases engagement and reduces friction. Consider adding WhatsApp access points in the following locations:

| Channel | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Mobile app** | Embed a WhatsApp link or button (e.g., on a product details page) |
| **Physical store** | Display a WhatsApp QR code or link at a prominent location |
| **Website** | Add a WhatsApp chat widget to the sidebar, footer, or specific pages |
| **Social media** | Add a WhatsApp button or link to your Facebook or Instagram profile |
| **CRM / helpdesk** | Integrate WhatsApp into your existing business tools |

Benefits of multi-channel access points:

- Reduces customer wait time and steps to reach support
- Increases conversion by enabling communication at the moment of intent
- Consolidates customer communication records across channels for better context
- Increases brand exposure across touchpoints
