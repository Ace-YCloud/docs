## Prerequisites

Before starting, make sure you have the following:

- A Facebook account
- A phone number that:
  - Can receive voice calls or SMS
  - Has **not** been previously registered with WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business App

<Note>
If you already have a Business Manager (BM) account, you can use your existing BM account during the embedded sign-up process.
</Note>

## Step 1: Create a WhatsApp Business API account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to YCloud">
    Visit the [YCloud console](https://www.ycloud.com/console/?redirect=/edit/getting-started#/app/dashboard/getStarted) and sign in or register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start embedded sign-up">
    On the Getting Started page, click the embedded sign-up button to begin linking your Facebook account and creating a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your account">
    After the sign-up flow completes, you can view your new account under **WhatsApp Account** in the YCloud console.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Complete business verification

Business verification is required to unlock higher messaging limits and register more phone numbers.

| BM account status | Max phone numbers | Daily conversation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Unverified | 2 | 250 |
| Verified | Up to 20 (extendable to 120) | Grows automatically |

### Prerequisites for verification

Prepare the following materials and ensure they are authentic and consistent with each other:

- Basic business information: company name, address, phone number, official website
- Business license or registration certificate

<Warning>
Your company's official website footer must display the same company name and information as shown on your business license.
</Warning>

### Verification process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Business Manager Security Center">
    As an admin of your BM account, navigate to the [Business Manager Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security) and initiate verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your documents">
    Upload your business license or registration certificate. The document must include your company's full legal name and address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the result">
    Verification typically completes within 1–2 working days. You will receive an email notification when a decision is made.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What do I do if I encounter errors during embedded sign-up?">
    Refer to the [Facebook embedded sign-up error documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/embedded-signup/errors?locale=en_US) for specific reasons and solutions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my business license does not include a company phone number?">
    You can submit a utility bill or operator invoice that includes the company's phone number as supplementary documentation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
