YCloud supports voice calling in two forms: **outbound calling** through YCloud's calling product, and **WhatsApp calling** embedded directly in the Inbox interface.

## Outbound calling

YCloud's calling product lets your agents make outbound phone calls to customers from within the platform. Calls are logged automatically and linked to the relevant contact record.

### Quick start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable calling for your account">
    Contact the YCloud support team to enable the calling feature. Once enabled, the **Calling** section appears in your navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your calling settings (admin)">
    Navigate to **Calling > Admin Guide** to set up call routing, assign agents to calling queues, and configure business hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the agent environment">
    Each agent who will make calls needs a headset or speaker/microphone. The calling interface is browser-based and does not require a separate application.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make an outbound call">
    Navigate to **Calling**, find the contact you want to call, and click **Call**. For bulk outbound calling campaigns, use the Outbound Calling guide.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Outbound calling campaigns

Outbound calling campaigns let you dial a list of contacts automatically, connecting answered calls to available agents.

To create an outbound calling campaign:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your contact list">
    Create a contact segment or upload the list of phone numbers you want to call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the campaign">
    Navigate to **Calling > Outbound Calling**, click **New Campaign**, and configure:
    - The contact list or segment to dial
    - The calling queue or agent group to route answered calls to
    - The calling schedule (days and hours)
    - The maximum number of simultaneous calls
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the campaign">
    Click **Start** to begin dialling. You can pause or stop the campaign at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Call logs

All calls — inbound and outbound — are recorded in **Calling > Calling Logs**. Each log entry shows the contact, agent, call duration, outcome, and timestamp.

## WhatsApp calling in Inbox

WhatsApp calling allows agents to initiate or receive WhatsApp voice calls directly from the Inbox interface, without switching to a phone.

<Note>
WhatsApp calling in Inbox is a separate feature from outbound calling. It uses the WhatsApp Business API and is available to contacts who also use WhatsApp on their device.
</Note>

To use WhatsApp calling in Inbox:

1. Open a conversation in **Inbox**.
2. Click the phone icon in the conversation toolbar.
3. The call connects through the customer's WhatsApp app.

WhatsApp calls are logged alongside the conversation history, so agents and supervisors have a complete record of all interactions with a contact.

## Roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Admin | Full access to calling configuration, queues, campaigns, and logs. |
| Agent | Can make and receive calls; can view their own call logs. |

For a full guide on admin configuration, see the [Admin Guide](https://helpdocs.ycloud.com/help-center/calling/manager-guideline) in the original YCloud help center. For agent-specific instructions, see the [Agent Guide](https://helpdocs.ycloud.com/help-center/calling/agent-guideline).
