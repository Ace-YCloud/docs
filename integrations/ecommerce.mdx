YCloud's e-commerce integration connects your online store to your WhatsApp communication flows. Once connected, you can automatically notify customers about order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery status — and handle inbound support queries in Inbox.

## Shopify integration

Shopify is the primary supported e-commerce platform. Connecting Shopify lets you:

- Trigger WhatsApp message templates automatically when order events occur (order placed, shipped, delivered, cancelled).
- Sync customer data between Shopify and your YCloud contact database.
- Route customer replies to your Inbox for agent follow-up.

### Connect your Shopify store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the E-commerce settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > E-commerce** in your YCloud account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Shopify">
    Click **Connect Shopify Store**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Enter your Shopify store domain (e.g. `yourstore.myshopify.com`) and follow the OAuth flow to grant YCloud the required permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map events to templates">
    For each order event you want to automate, select the WhatsApp message template to send. Templates must be pre-approved before you can select them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the integration">
    Toggle the integration on and save. YCloud will begin sending notifications for new orders immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Message templates used for order notifications must be of category **Utility** (for transactional messages such as order confirmations and shipping updates). Make sure your templates are approved before enabling the integration.
</Note>

## Other e-commerce platforms

If you use a different e-commerce platform, you can achieve similar functionality using:

- **Webhooks** — Configure your platform to send order events to a YCloud webhook URL, then use the API to trigger the appropriate message template.
- **n8n** — Build a no-code workflow that connects your e-commerce platform to YCloud via the n8n integration.

See [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks) and [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview) for details.

## Order notifications with WhatsApp Pay

If you are using YCloud's WhatsApp Pay feature, you can also send order detail messages with embedded checkout buttons, allowing customers to complete purchases directly within WhatsApp.

## Coupons

YCloud supports sending personalised coupon codes in campaign messages. Coupon codes can be imported or generated and embedded in message templates as variables. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Coupons** to manage your coupon pools.
