---
title: "Segments"
description: "How to filter contacts into groups using saved segments, and how to use those segments as the audience for a campaign."
---

Segments are saved filter views of your contact list. You define a set of conditions — for example, "country is Brazil" and "tag is VIP" — and YCloud shows all contacts that match. You can save the view as a named segment that persists in your sidebar.

Segments are dynamic: as new contacts are added or existing contacts are updated, they are automatically included in or excluded from the segment based on your filter conditions.

## Create a segment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Contact list">
    Navigate to **Contact > Contact list**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a filter">
    Click **Add filter** at the top of the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a filter type">
    Choose the attribute you want to filter on — for example, **Country**, **Tag**, **Owner**, or any custom attribute you have defined.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the filter value">
    Select or type the value to match. For example, if you chose **Country**, select **Brazil**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add more conditions (optional)">
    Click **Add filter** again to stack additional conditions. All conditions must be satisfied for a contact to appear (AND logic).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the results">
    Click **Done**. The contact list refreshes to show only matching contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the segment">
    Click **Save Segment**, enter a name, and save. The segment appears in the left sidebar under the contact list navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filterable fields

You can filter on any of the following:

- Phone number
- Name / nickname
- Country
- Tags
- Owner
- Subscription status
- Any custom attribute defined in Contact Settings

## Use a segment in a campaign

When creating a WhatsApp marketing campaign, you select the audience at the **Recipients** step. Saved segments are available as audience options.

<Tip>
Create segments before you build your campaign. This makes the campaign setup faster and ensures your audience is consistent across multiple sends.
</Tip>

<Note>
Contacts on the Unsubscribe list are automatically excluded from campaigns even if they match the segment filter — you do not need to manage this manually.
</Note>

## Manage segments

Segments are listed in the left sidebar of the Contact list. You can:

- Click any segment to view its contacts.
- Edit the segment name or filter conditions by selecting the segment and modifying the filters.
- Delete a segment by hovering over it in the sidebar and selecting the delete option.

Deleting a segment does not delete the underlying contacts.
