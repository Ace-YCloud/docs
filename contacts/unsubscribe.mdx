The unsubscribe list holds phone numbers that have opted out of receiving marketing messages from your business. YCloud automatically excludes these contacts from future campaigns — you do not need to filter them out manually.

## How opt-outs work

A contact can be added to your unsubscribe list in two ways:

1. **Automatically** — YCloud can detect opt-out keywords in incoming WhatsApp messages (such as "STOP", "Unsubscribe", or equivalents in other languages) and add the sender to the unsubscribe list automatically. This requires a chatbot or automation to be configured for keyword detection.

2. **Manually** — You or your team can add a phone number directly from the **Contact > Unsubscribe** page.

<Warning>
Failing to honour opt-out requests increases your complaint rate. A high complaint rate degrades your WhatsApp phone number quality score and can result in reduced messaging limits or account suspension. Always respect opt-outs promptly.
</Warning>

## Manually add an unsubscribed contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Unsubscribe page">
    Go to **Contact > Unsubscribe**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the phone number">
    Click **+ Add Phone Number**, enter the phone number in international format, and click **Add**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Delete a contact from the unsubscribe list

If a contact requests to be re-added to your marketing list (re-subscribes), you can remove them from the unsubscribe list.

Navigate to **Contact > Unsubscribe**, find the phone number in the list, and click **Action > Delete**.

<Note>
Deleting a contact from the unsubscribe list does not re-subscribe them automatically. Make sure you have documented the contact's re-subscription consent before removing them.
</Note>

## Export the unsubscribe list

Navigate to **Contact > Unsubscribe** and click **Export** to download the full list as a CSV file.

## Opt-in and opt-out compliance

### Collecting opt-ins

WhatsApp requires businesses to obtain consent (opt-in) before sending marketing messages. Common opt-in methods include:

- A consent checkbox on your website or app during registration
- An SMS or in-app message asking the customer to confirm
- A paper form signed by the customer
- An inbound WhatsApp conversation initiated by the customer
- An Interactive Voice Response (IVR) flow

The most effective approach is to prompt users for consent at the point where they register or interact with your product, so they have a clear expectation of receiving messages.

### Handling opt-outs

<Tip>
Include opt-out instructions in the first marketing message a customer receives from you, and again after a long gap between messages. You do not need to include it in every message.
</Tip>

Best practices for opt-outs:

- **Respect opt-out requests immediately.** All your WhatsApp numbers should stop sending marketing messages to opted-out contacts.
- **Send a confirmation reply.** Acknowledge the opt-out and optionally ask for feedback on why the customer chose to unsubscribe.
- **Do not include opt-out instructions in every message.** Doing so can prompt unnecessary opt-outs. Include them selectively — in your first message, in the first message after a long gap, or if sending to previously unsubscribed contacts.

### Why complaint rate matters

When users block or report your business account, WhatsApp records a complaint. If your complaint rate falls below Meta's quality threshold over time, your account will face sending restrictions. In severe cases the account can be banned. Proactively managing opt-outs is the primary way to keep your complaint rate low.
