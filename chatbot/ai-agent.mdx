## What is the AI agent?

The AI agent (reactive AI agent) responds to inbound customer messages by assuming a predefined role. By carefully setting up the agent's profile, providing comprehensive guidelines, and enriching its knowledge base and action guides, it becomes capable of handling common, repetitive inquiries as well as complex data-interaction tasks. This significantly boosts the efficiency of your customer service operations.

Unlike a rule-based bot, the AI agent understands natural language — customers do not need to send exact keywords to get a response.

## Configuration

The AI agent configuration is organized into three sections: **General**, **Knowledge**, and **Manuals**.

### General

#### Profile

Set the personality and conversation rules of the chatbot to meet the requirements of your brand's customer service.

In Profile, you can set the avatar and name of the AI agent, and choose a role:

  <Accordion title="Lead acquisition">

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Shop assistant">
    Guides customers to their desired products instantly. Can answer questions about features, availability, pricing, and promotions. Provides personalized recommendations and suggests complementary items based on customer needs.

  <Accordion title="Customer service">

    Define a custom role tailored to your specific business needs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Knowledge

The Knowledge section is where you provide the material the AI uses to answer customer questions.

#### Knowledge base

Enable the AI to learn from documents, FAQs, and other content you upload. A well-populated knowledge base directly improves the quality of AI answers to customer questions.

<Tip>
  Review unresolved queries regularly and add the relevant knowledge to cover them. This reduces escalations to human agents and lets your team focus on business growth.
</Tip>

#### Suggestions

Provide additional guidance for questions the AI has not yet answered well. Adding suggestions for unaddressed queries improves the AI's ability to respond to those specific topics.

### Manuals

#### Flows

Build visual conversation flows in the flow editor and let the chatbot interact with customers on WhatsApp using structured, predictable paths for defined scenarios.

#### Actionbooks

Break down complex processes into steps and teach the AI how to manage them. Actionbooks let you define multi-step tasks — such as querying an external database or updating a record — that the AI can execute within a conversation.

<Note>
  Actionbooks extend the AI beyond simple Q&A into actual business process automation, enabling interactions with your external systems.
</Note>
