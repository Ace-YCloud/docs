## Creating a chatbot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Chatbot section">
    Log in to YCloud and navigate to **ChatBot**. Click **Create Chatbot** and select the type of chatbot you want to create.

    <Note>
      The configuration options vary depending on the type you choose. See [AI Agent](/chatbot/ai-agent) or the rule-based agent docs for type-specific settings.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the basic configuration">
    Fill in the required settings for your chosen bot type. This includes the bot name and any type-specific options such as the AI role or keyword triggers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the chatbot to a WhatsApp number">
    It is recommended to complete the chatbot configuration before this step.

    Navigate to **WhatsApp accounts** > **Settings** > **Assignment**.

    Under **Priority 3 assign to**, select **Chatbot**, then choose the chatbot you want to assign from the second dropdown.

    <Tip>
      Assigning the chatbot after finishing its configuration ensures the bot is ready to handle incoming conversations immediately upon activation.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Copying a chatbot

If you want to reuse an existing chatbot as a starting point:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the chatbot">
    In the **ChatBot** list, find the chatbot you want to copy and click the **Copy** button on the right side of that row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the copy configuration">
    In the pop-up window, enter a name for the copied chatbot and complete any other configuration fields shown. The specific fields depend on the type of chatbot being copied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The copied chatbot starts as an independent bot. Changes to the copy do not affect the original.
</Note>
