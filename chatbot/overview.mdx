Chatbot is a powerful tool that integrates both AI-powered intelligent agents and rule-based workflow bots. It can handle a wide range of tasks, from simple keyword-triggered responses to complex operations like order inquiries and updating external databases. By automating repetitive customer interactions and well-defined procedures, it significantly boosts your support team's efficiency and reduces costs.

## Bot types

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="AI Agent" icon="robot">
    Responds to inbound messages using natural language understanding. You configure a role, knowledge base, and action guides — the AI handles the rest. Ideal for open-ended conversations, FAQs, lead capture, and complex data-interaction tasks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rule-based agent" icon="diagram-project">
    Follows a fixed flow triggered by keywords. When a customer sends a matching keyword, the bot executes a predefined sequence of steps. Predictable and easy to audit.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Key concepts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Trigger">
    A keyword trigger. When a user starts a conversation with your WhatsApp account and sends a keyword that matches a configured trigger, the corresponding chatbot flow activates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Chatbot flow">
    The process that defines how a chatbot interacts with users and handles their inputs — the sequence of messages, decisions, and actions the bot follows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Variables">
    Variables store and track information during a conversation. By using variables, the chatbot can adjust automated replies dynamically based on different situations and user inputs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data and insights

By tracking the number of times a chatbot is triggered and the interactions between customers and the bot, you can collect data on frequent questions and customer needs. This provides valuable insights for further product and process optimization.
