---
title: "Campaigns"
description: "Create and send WhatsApp marketing campaigns, schedule sends, track button clicks, and analyze delivery and engagement data."
---

Campaigns let you send approved WhatsApp message templates to a list of contacts in bulk. You can send immediately or schedule for a future time, personalize messages with variables, and track delivery and engagement results.

<Note>
  WhatsApp campaigns require Meta-approved message templates. Sending unsolicited promotional messages to users who have not opted in can result in account suspension. Always obtain proper consent before messaging contacts.
</Note>

## Creating a campaign

### Step 1: Create a marketing template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Templates">
    Log in to YCloud and navigate to **Home** > **Templates** > **+ Add Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the category and name">
    Select **Marketing** as the category. Enter a template name and choose a language.

    <Warning>
      Template names must be unique and can only contain lowercase letters (a–z), digits (0–9), and underscores. Once submitted, the name cannot be changed.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the message content">
    Marketing messages can include:
    - Promotional or discount offers
    - Welcome or closing messages (e.g., "Thank you for shopping at [store name]")
    - Updates, invitations, or event announcements

    Add call-to-action buttons or quick reply buttons to make your message actionable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for approval">
    Click **Submit** and wait for Meta to approve the template before using it in a campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Create a campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaign">
    Navigate to **Campaign** > **+ Add Campaign** > **WhatsApp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in campaign details">
    Complete the following fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | Campaign name | Enter a name, or use the default |
    | Sender | The phone number connected through the embedded sign-up process |
    | Template | Select the Meta-approved template and language |
    | Send time | Send immediately or schedule a future date and time |

    Click **Next** to proceed to recipient selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose recipients">
    YCloud offers three ways to specify recipients:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="Manual input">
        Enter phone numbers directly in the box, one per line in international format (e.g., +447759398257 for a UK number).

        **Limitation:** When using manual input with a template that has variables, all recipients receive the same fixed variable value. For personalized variables, use CSV upload instead.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Upload CSV">
        Download the CSV template, fill in phone numbers in the first column (named "phone"), and add variable columns starting from the second column if your template uses variables.

        For example, if your template says "Hi, \{\{name\}\}", add a column named "name" with each recipient's value.

        CSV upload is the recommended method when your template contains variables.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Contact filter">
        Select recipients from your existing YCloud contacts using one of two filter options:

        - **Contact segment:** Choose one or more saved segments (up to 10 simultaneously).
        - **Contact attributes:** Add filter criteria based on contact properties/traits. Contacts matching all criteria become the campaign recipients.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set variables">
    If your template includes a media header or body variables, configure them in this step:

    - **Headers:** Replace the default example media with your own to improve review chances.
    - **Variables:** Upload your file with variable data and map each template variable to the corresponding column. Alternatively, set a variable to a fixed text value that applies to all recipients.

    If your template has no variables, this step is skipped automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the campaign details and preview the message. Click **Submit** to complete the campaign submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tracking button clicks

You can track clicks on the "Visit Website" button in utility and marketing templates.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable button tracking in the template">
    When creating or editing a template, locate the button settings and enable **Button tracking** for the "Visit Website" button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the campaign">
    Create and send your campaign using the template with button tracking enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View click data">
    After the campaign runs, navigate to the campaign's analytics page to view button click counts and related data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Button tracking is useful for measuring how many recipients clicked through to your website, helping you calculate conversion rates from WhatsApp campaigns.
</Tip>

## Analyzing campaign data

After a campaign completes, view delivery and engagement data in the backend.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open campaign analytics">
    Navigate to **Campaign** > find your campaign > **Action** > **Analytics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review metrics and export">
    The analytics page shows the following data:

    | Status | Explanation |
    |---|---|
    | Sent | Total number of messages sent for the campaign |
    | Processing | Messages sent but not yet successfully received — may be due to network issues or the app not being opened |
    | Delivered | Messages successfully received by the recipient's device |
    | Failed | Messages that failed to send; click the **?** next to Failed to see specific failure reasons |
    | Read | Messages that have been read by the end user |
    | Replied | Messages replied to by the end user within 24 hours of successful receipt |

    Click **Export** to download the data for further analysis in your own tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>
