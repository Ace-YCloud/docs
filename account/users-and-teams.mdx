title: "Users & Teams"
description: "How to invite team members, assign roles, manage agents, and organise your team within YCloud."
---

YCloud supports multiple users within a single account. Admins can invite team members, assign them roles, and organise them into teams for routing and reporting purposes.

## Roles

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access to all settings, billing, integrations, and team management. Can view and act on all conversations in Inbox. |
| **Agent** | Can use Inbox to handle conversations, manage contacts, and initiate chats. Cannot access billing or account-level settings. |

<Note>
Some features — such as the Calling admin guide, managing message templates, and configuring chatbots — are restricted to Admin users.
</Note>

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Users and Teams">
    Navigate to **Settings > Account Management > Users and Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite a new user">
    Click **Invite User** and enter the email address of the person you want to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a role">
    Select **Admin** or **Agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Click **Send Invite**. The invited user will receive an email with a link to set up their account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage existing users

From the **Users and Teams** page you can:

- **Change a user's role** — Click the user's name and update their role.
- **Deactivate a user** — Deactivated users lose access to the account but their conversation history and contact assignments are preserved.
- **Reassign contacts** — If an agent leaves, use the Contact list to bulk-reassign their contacts to another owner.

## Teams

Teams group agents together so that conversations can be assigned to a team rather than an individual. When a conversation is assigned to a team, any agent in that team can pick it up.

To create a team:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Teams settings">
    Navigate to **Settings > Account Management > Users and Teams** and select the **Teams** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a team">
    Click **New Team**, give it a name (e.g. `Sales`, `Support`, `Billing`), and add the agents who belong to this team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The team is now available for conversation routing and owner assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Agent availability

Agents can set their availability status (online / away / offline) within the Inbox interface. Conversation auto-assignment rules take agent availability into account — conversations are only routed to agents who are currently online.

## Personal preferences

Each user can configure their own notification settings, language, timezone, and other personal preferences at **Settings > Personal Preferences Settings**. These settings only affect their own experience and do not impact other team members.
