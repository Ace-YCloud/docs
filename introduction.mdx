## Get started in 4 steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your WhatsApp Business API account">
    Use the embedded sign-up flow to connect your Facebook Business Manager and register a phone number. [Create account →](/quick-start/create-whatsapp-account)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your business">
    Complete Meta's business verification to unlock higher messaging limits and more phone numbers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a message template">
    Design and submit a WhatsApp message template for Meta approval. Templates are required for outbound messages. [Learn about templates →](/whatsapp-basics/message-templates)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your first message">
    Use the YCloud API or dashboard to send your first WhatsApp message. [API reference →](/api-reference/introduction)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  YCloud is a Meta-official Premier WhatsApp Business Solution Provider. All messaging is powered by the WhatsApp Business API.
</Note>
