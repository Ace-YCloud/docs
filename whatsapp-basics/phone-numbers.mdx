A WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) requires at least one valid phone number to send and receive messages.

## Adding a phone number

If you do not have a WABA yet, create one through the embedded sign-up process. Your first phone number is connected during this flow.

If you already have a WABA, add additional numbers via **YCloud > WhatsApp Account > Add number**.

## Migrating a phone number

If you want to use a number already registered on the WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business platform:

- [Migrate an existing WhatsApp number to a business account](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/on-premises/get-started/migrate-existing-whatsapp-number-to-a-business-account)
- Migrate a phone number to another WABA

## Registration limits

| BM account status | Max phone numbers |
|---|---|
| Unverified | 2 |
| Business verified | Up to 20 |
| Extended (on request) | Up to 120 |

Contact the YCloud support team if you need more than 20 numbers.

## Display name

Each phone number has a display name — the brand name shown to customers in WhatsApp. The display name must follow [Meta's Display Name Guidelines](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/757569725593362) and is reviewed by Meta before going live.

To change your display name: **YCloud > WhatsApp accounts > Number settings > Business Profile > Display name > Edit**.

**Display name rules:**
- Must be 3–150 characters
- Must be related to your company or brand name

**Display name statuses after submission:**
- **Approved** — The name passed review and is live.
- **In review** — The name is currently being reviewed.
- **Rejected** — The name did not pass review.

## Phone number status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | Sending is working normally. |
| **Flagged** | The quality rating has dropped to Low. You cannot upgrade your message limit tier. If quality improves to Medium or High within 7 days, the status returns to Connected. If not, the number returns to Connected but at a lower limit tier. |
| **Restricted** | The number has reached its daily message limit. You cannot initiate new business-initiated conversations until the 24-hour window resets. You can still reply to user-initiated messages. |

## Quality rating

Your quality rating reflects how receptive recipients are to your messages. It is based on signals such as blocks and reports from users.

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Almost no negative customer feedback |
| **Medium** | Some negative feedback |
| **Low** | Negative feedback from multiple customers |

View quality at **YCloud > WhatsApp accounts**.

<Tip>
To maintain a high quality rating:

1. Only message customers who have opted in.
2. Send personalized, relevant messages — avoid generic broadcasts.
3. Control message frequency.
4. Always provide an opt-out method (e.g., a "stop" or "unsubscribe" button).
5. Respect opt-outs and do not message unsubscribed users.
</Tip>

## Message limits

Message limits control the maximum number of business-initiated conversations you can open in a rolling 24-hour period. They do not apply to user-initiated conversations.

### Starting limits

| Condition | Daily limit |
|---|---|
| Unverified BM, or display name not yet approved | 250 conversations |
| Business verified + display name approved | 1,000 conversations |

### Increasing limits

Limits can increase automatically through the following tiers:

- 1,000 business-initiated conversations
- 10,000 business-initiated conversations
- 100,000 business-initiated conversations
- Unlimited business-initiated conversations

**Automatic expansion criteria** — Each time you initiate a new conversation, WhatsApp evaluates whether to increase your limit. All of the following must be true:

1. Phone number status is **Connected**
2. Quality rating is **Medium** or **High**
3. In the past 7 days, you have initiated conversations with at least X unique customers, where X equals your current limit divided by 2

If all conditions are met, the limit increases one tier within 24 hours.

## Removing a phone number

Go to **BM Account > WhatsApp Manager > Phone numbers**, find the number, and click the delete icon.

**Deletion rules:**
- Only BM account admins can delete numbers.
- A number cannot be deleted if paid messages were sent using it in the past 30 days.
- After deletion, the number can be re-registered on WhatsApp or the WhatsApp Business Platform (unless it was banned by the platform).
