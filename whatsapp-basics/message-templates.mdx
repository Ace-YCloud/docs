Message templates are pre-approved message formats used to initiate marketing, utility, and authentication conversations with customers. Unlike free-form messages, templates are the only type of message that can be sent to customers who have not messaged you or have not messaged you in the past 24 hours.

Templates must be approved by Meta before they can be sent. They may also be automatically suspended based on customer feedback.

## Template categories

Each template belongs to one of three categories, which determines how the conversation is billed:

| Category | Use case |
|---|---|
| **Authentication** | Send one-time passwords and verification codes |
| **Utility** | Send account updates, order confirmations, alerts, and other transactional information |
| **Marketing** | Send promotional offers, product announcements, and other marketing content |

## Template structure

A message template can include the following sections:

| Section | Description |
|---|---|
| **Header** | Optional. Can contain a logo, brand element, title, or video. |
| **Body** | Required. The main message content. Should be clear and concise. |
| **Footer** | Optional. Additional information such as a call to action or contact details. |
| **Buttons** | Optional. Encourage recipients to take a specific action, such as visiting a URL or calling a number. |

## Creating templates

**In the YCloud dashboard:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the template editor">
    Go to **WhatsApp Manager > Templates > Add Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select category, name, and language">
    Choose the category (Marketing, Utility, or Authentication), enter a unique template name (lowercase letters a–z, 0–9, and underscores only — cannot be changed after submission), and select the language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit content">
    Fill in the header, body, footer, and any buttons. For templates with variables, use the format `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`, etc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Review the content and click **Submit > Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Via the API:** Refer to the [WhatsApp Template Creation Examples](https://docs.ycloud.com/reference/whatsapp-template-creation-examples).

## Template review

After submission, approval can take up to 24 hours. The BM account administrator receives an email notification once a decision is made.

- **Approved** — Status is set to **Active - Quality Pending**. You can start sending immediately.
- **Rejected** — You can edit and resubmit the template, or file an appeal.

## Common reasons for rejection

Avoid the following issues to improve approval chances:

- Missing or mismatched variable parameters (correct format: `{{1}}`)
- Variable parameters containing special characters such as `#`, `$`, or `%`
- Non-consecutive variable numbering (e.g., `{{1}}`, `{{2}}`, `{{4}}` with `{{3}}` missing)
- Content that violates [WhatsApp's business policies](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-policy/) or [commerce policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/commerce-policy/)
- Requesting sensitive identifiers from users (e.g., full payment card numbers, national ID numbers)
- Abusive or threatening content
- Duplicate of an existing template (same body and footer text)

<Note>
Authentication templates are exempt from the duplicate content check.
</Note>

## Template statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In Review** | Template is pending review (up to 24 hours) |
| **Rejected** | Template was rejected during review |
| **Active - Quality Pending** | Approved and ready to send; no quality feedback yet |
| **Active - High Quality** | Very little negative feedback; safe to continue sending |
| **Active - Medium Quality** | Some negative feedback; may be suspended soon |
| **Active - Low Quality** | Significant negative feedback; at risk of suspension |
| **Suspended** | Temporarily suspended due to repeated negative feedback; cannot be sent |
| **Disabled** | Permanently disabled due to repeated negative feedback |
| **Appeal - In Review** | An appeal has been submitted and is under review |

## Template suspension

If users repeatedly block or report messages from a template, WhatsApp pauses it temporarily:

- **1st report:** 3-hour suspension
- **2nd report:** 6-hour suspension
- **3rd report:** Disabled

During suspension, you can:

- **Edit the template content** — Remove words or phrases that may be perceived as spam or phishing. Updates take effect within two minutes; you do not need to wait for the suspension period to end.
- **Reshape the target audience** — Send the template only to the most relevant segment of your contacts.
- **Take no action** — The template resumes after the suspension period, but may be suspended again if the issues persist.

## Template appeal

If your template is rejected, you can file an appeal through your BM account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Account Quality">
    In Business Manager, navigate to [Account Quality](https://business.facebook.com/business-support-home/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find rejected templates">
    Click **Rejected Message Templates** and select the template you want to appeal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the appeal">
    Click **Request Review**, enter your appeal reason with supporting examples, and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the decision">
    The appeal review decision is typically communicated within 24–48 hours via Business Manager. The outcome will show as **Unchanged** or **Reversed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
