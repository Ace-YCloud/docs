## What are WhatsApp Flows?

WhatsApp Flows is a framework for building structured, interactive experiences within WhatsApp messages. With Flows, you can define, configure, and customize messages with rich UI elements — such as forms, selection menus, and multi-step interactions — that allow customers to complete tasks without leaving the WhatsApp app.

YCloud supports sending WhatsApp Flows as part of template and session messages.

## Common scenarios

### Get leads for pre-approved loans

Designed for the financial services sector, this Flow type simplifies the borrowing process:

- Present a loan amount to the user, with the ability to customize loan terms such as the repayment period.
- Allow the user to select their preferred disbursement and payment method.
- Include user authentication to confirm identity.

This pattern can be adapted for credit card offers, loan calculators, loan offers for shopping, and full loan qualification flows.

### Personalized offer

This Flow type engages customers during promotional events:

- Users select products they are interested in and provide their budget.
- The Flow surfaces a specific product recommendation based on their inputs.
- You can follow up with a message containing purchase instructions.

### Collect purchase intent

This Flow type helps businesses understand what customers want ahead of marketing and sales campaigns:

- Collect personal data from the customer, such as name and email address.
- Allow the customer to select the products or services they are interested in.

This pattern is adaptable to any use case where you need to gather structured information from users.
