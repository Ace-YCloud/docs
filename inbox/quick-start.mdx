The Inbox interface is divided into four main areas. Understanding what each one does will help you navigate conversations efficiently.

- **Search and filtering area (left panel, top)** — Set your agent status, search for conversations by name or phone number, and apply quick or advanced filters.
- **Conversation list (left panel, below search)** — Lists all conversations. Click any conversation to open it.
- **Chat area (center)** — The main communication area. Displays the full conversation history and contains the message input area.
- **Customer details (right panel)** — Shows the contact's name, profile, tags, and attributes. You can edit these directly from here.

<Note>
Because Inbox is linked to all WhatsApp numbers in your YCloud account, the same customer may have multiple conversation windows. Check the Inbox name next to the customer's name at the top of the chat area to confirm which number a conversation belongs to.
</Note>

## Search and filtering

### Basic search

Inbox supports searching historical conversations by **phone number or name**. Text-based content search is not currently supported.

To search: click the search box at the top left of Inbox, then enter the name or number.

### Quick filter

Filter conversations by assignment status, contact tags, or custom filters. Each filter category supports single selection only.

### Advanced filter

Advanced filtering lets you combine multiple conditions to narrow down conversations. Conditions use an "AND" relationship — all must match.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open advanced filter">
    Click the add ("+") button on the left side of the filter area. The advanced filter panel appears automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions">
    Select the attributes you want to filter by (for example: agent, tag, or date), set the values, then click "Add filter" to include more conditions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View results">
    Matching conversations appear in the conversation list immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a condition">
    Hover over the condition and click the cross icon on the right to delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the filter">
    Click the advanced filter button again to close the panel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Bulk transfer and close

You can transfer or close multiple conversations at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select conversations">
    Hover over conversations in the list. A checkbox appears on the left side of each item. Check all the conversations you want to act on. Press **Esc** to cancel your selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer or close">
    Use the **Transfer** or **Close** button that appears in the upper right of the conversation list. A confirmation dialog appears before the action is applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Chat area

### Conversation display

A **conversation** is the full exchange between you and a contact. A **message** is a single item within that exchange.

**Conversation statuses:**
- **Open** — The conversation is active.
- **Closed** — The conversation has ended. Conversations automatically close after 24 hours of no reply from the customer.

**Message delivery statuses:**
- Sent — message successfully sent.
- Delivered — message delivered to the customer's device.
- Read — message has been read.
- Send error — hover over the message to see the error reason.

**Message actions:** Hover over any message to reveal the three-dot menu. From there you can:
- **Quote (reply)** — attach the original message to your reply for context.
- **React** — add an emoji reaction to a message.

### Message input area

When a conversation is assigned to you, the input area supports:

1. Adding emojis
2. Attaching files
3. Sending a WhatsApp template message

**Using canned responses:** Type `/` at the beginning of the input box (not after other text) to browse all preset quick replies. Use the arrow keys to navigate, then press **Enter** to insert the response.

**Sending:** Press **Enter** or click the **Send** button.

## Customer details

Click the **Expand** button or the customer's avatar to open the customer details panel. From here you can:

- **Rename the contact** — click the edit icon next to the name.
- **Reassign the conversation** — click the assignment dropdown and select an agent or team.
- **Add tags** — click the "+" button in the Tags section to apply existing tags.
- **Edit attributes** — click the text next to any attribute to edit it. Click away or press **Enter** to save.

## Conversation operations

**Star a customer** — Click the star icon to mark a high-value customer as a favorite. Starred customers appear under the "Starred" label in the left panel.

**Transfer a conversation** — Click the **Transfer** button at the top of the chat area and select the agent to transfer to. You can search by name.

**Close a conversation** — Click the **Close** button at the top of the chat area. The conversation status changes to "Closed".
