---
title: "AI Copilot"
description: "Use AI to automatically summarize conversations on transfer or closure, reducing manual effort for agents and supervisors."
---

AI Copilot provides AI-powered conversation summaries. It extracts structured information from conversation content to help agents quickly understand context when taking over a conversation, and helps supervisors review closed conversations without reading through the full history.

## AI summary

Conversation transfers happen frequently — between human agents, and from chatbot to human. When you take over a transferred conversation, you need to understand what was discussed before you can continue helping the customer. Similarly, supervisors reviewing closed conversations need a structured picture of what happened.

AI Copilot addresses both scenarios by generating structured summaries from the conversation content. The AI automatically determines whether the conversation is a pre-sales or post-sales scenario and generates an appropriate summary accordingly.

<Note>
AI summaries require AI Credits. If your balance is insufficient, summary generation will fail. Check your balance under your account settings.
</Note>

## Summary on conversation transfer

This applies when a conversation is transferred between human agents, or from a chatbot to a human agent.

### Manual summary

When a conversation transfer occurs, the receiving agent sees an **AI Summary** button next to the system transfer log entry in the conversation.

Click **AI Summary** to trigger a summary of the conversation content up to that point. Once generated:

- Click **AI Summary** again to regenerate.
- Click **Noted** to collapse and dismiss the summary.

### Automatic summary on transfer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Inbox settings">
    Click the gear icon on the right side of the Inbox header to enter the Inbox settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable auto-transfer summary">
    Toggle on **Transfer automatic summary**. The AI will now generate a summary automatically every time a conversation is transferred, without requiring the agent to click the button manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Summary on conversation closure

This applies when supervisors want to review what happened in a completed conversation.

### Manual summary

At the end of a conversation, click the **AI Summary** button in the conversation to generate a summary for that session.

### Automatic summary on closure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open global settings">
    Go to **Settings** in the lower-left corner of the page, then navigate to **Inbox > AI Copilot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable auto-closure summary">
    Toggle on **Automatic summary when session is closed**. This setting applies globally — all conversations in Inbox will automatically generate an AI summary when closed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use automatic closure summaries when supervisors regularly audit conversation quality. The summaries provide a fast way to review agent performance without reading every message.
</Tip>
