title: "Calling"
description: "Make and receive WhatsApp voice calls with customers directly from Inbox."
---

YCloud integrates WhatsApp voice calling into the Inbox, so your team can switch from text to voice without leaving the conversation. Inbound calls from customers are free; outbound calls are chargeable.

## Invite a customer to call in

When a text conversation isn't enough, send the customer a call invitation directly from the Inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calling panel">
    In a conversation, click **WhatsApp Calling**, then choose **Ask customer to call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the call reason">
    Type a brief reason for the call, then click **Send**. The customer receives an invitation message with a **Call** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the incoming call">
    When the customer taps the Call button on their phone, you'll see an incoming call notification in the Inbox. Click **Answer** to start the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Inbound calls initiated by the customer via a call invitation are free of charge.
</Note>

## Make an outbound call

Before placing an outbound call, your agent must have calling permissions for the business phone number.

<Warning>
  Outbound voice calls are chargeable. Check [ycloud.com/pricing](https://www.ycloud.com/pricing) for current rates.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request calling permission">
    In the conversation, click **WhatsApp Calling**, then choose **Call out**. YCloud will guide you to first send a call request message to the customer asking for their permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for customer authorization">
    Once the customer approves the call request, you'll receive a system notification in the Inbox confirming the permission is granted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the call">
    After authorization is confirmed, click **Call out** again to initiate the outbound voice call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View call records

Open the **Call Records** panel on the right-hand side of the Inbox to see detailed logs for every call, including status, duration, and timestamps.

<Tip>
  You can also review full calling analytics in the dedicated Calling section. See [Calling](/integrations/calling) for more details.
</Tip>
