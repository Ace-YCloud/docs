This guide walks you through getting set up as an agent and using Inbox day-to-day.

## Getting started

### Accept the invitation

Find the activation email sent by your admin and click **Activate your account**. This completes your account setup. You do not need to register separately on the YCloud website.

### Set up your profile

Go to your avatar in the lower-left corner > **Edit** > **Account > Profile**.

Upload a profile image (recommended size 640×640, max 5 MB) and fill in your basic details. This information is used for internal identification only — customers see the profile image associated with the WhatsApp number, not your personal photo.

### Configure notifications

Go to **Account > Notifications** to control when you receive alerts:

- **Sound alerts** — choose which events trigger an audio notification.
- **Browser pop-up alerts** — configure browser notifications (you must grant browser permission for this to work).

## Setting your status

Your status controls whether new conversations are automatically assigned to you.

| Status | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Available** | New conversations are automatically assigned to you. You can also receive manual transfers. |
| **Away** | New conversations are not auto-assigned when assignment rules are set to "online agents only". You can still receive manual transfers. |
| **Offline (not logged in)** | Only Owner-priority rules will assign conversations to you. Manual transfers are still possible. |

<Warning>
After logging in, your status defaults to **Away**. Change it to **Available** before you start your shift so new conversations are routed to you.
</Warning>

## Starting a conversation

There are three ways to start a conversation with a customer.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Respond to inbound messages">
    When a customer sends you a message, it appears in your conversation list. Select the conversation to open it and reply directly in the chat area.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Transfer to yourself">
    If a conversation exists but is assigned to another agent or is unassigned, click the **Transfer to me** prompt in the chat box to take ownership of it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Start a new conversation">
    To reach a contact who has not messaged you first:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Search for the contact">
        Enter the customer's phone number in the search box.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create the conversation">
        Click **+ Create a new conversation** below the search results.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a WhatsApp number">
        Choose which of your business numbers to send from, then click **Next**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose a template">
        Select a WhatsApp message template to send as the opening message. You can filter templates by category.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set variables and send">
        Fill in any template variables, then click **Send**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    You must send a WhatsApp template to open a new conversation with a customer who has not messaged you. Free-form messages are only available within an open 24-hour messaging window.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Handling conversations

### Replying to messages

In the message input area you can:

- **Add emojis** using the emoji picker.
- **Attach files** (images, video, audio, documents).
- **Send a template** by clicking the template icon.
- **Use a canned response** by typing `/` at the start of the input box.

Send the message by pressing **Enter** or clicking the **Send** button.

### Using canned responses

Canned responses let you insert pre-written replies instantly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the canned response list">
    Type `/` at the beginning of the input box (it must be the first character, not following other text). A list of all available quick replies appears with a content preview on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a response">
    Click the response in the list, or type part of the shortcode to narrow it down. Use the **up/down arrow keys** to navigate, then press **Enter** to insert the text into the input box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit if needed and send">
    The response populates the input box. Edit it if necessary, then send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Quoting and reacting

Hover over any message to reveal the three-dot menu:

- **Quote (reply)** — attaches the original message to your reply so the customer knows exactly what you are responding to.
- **React** — add an emoji reaction (like, love, surprise, etc.) without sending a text reply.

### Starring a customer

Click the star icon on the conversation to mark a high-value customer as a favorite. Starred customers appear under the **Starred** label in the left panel for quick access.

### Transferring a conversation

Click the **Transfer** button at the top of the chat area, then search for and select the agent or team to transfer to.

### Closing a conversation

Click the **Close** button at the top of the chat area when the customer's issue is resolved. The conversation status changes to "Closed".

Conversations also close automatically if the customer does not respond within the period configured by your admin.

## Tagging conversations

Conversation tags help classify and filter conversations.

**On an open conversation:** Click the **Add Conversation Tag** button above the conversation display area. Select an existing tag or type a new one with custom text and color.

**On a closed conversation:** Scroll to the bottom of the closed conversation and click the **Add Conversation Tag** button.

To remove a tag, hover over it and click the delete symbol that appears.

<Note>
Only admins and Inbox managers can create and manage the shared tag list. Agents can apply existing tags or add ad-hoc custom tags directly in conversations.
</Note>

## Message translation

Inbox includes built-in translation to help you communicate with customers in different languages.

### Enable translation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open personal settings">
    Click your profile photo in the lower-left corner, then click the gear icon to open personal settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Translation">
    Select **Translation** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the switches">
    - **Input Translation** — translates what you type in the input box into the customer's language before sending.
    - **Automated Customer Message Translation** — automatically translates incoming customer messages into your preferred language. The default target language matches the system UI language but can be adjusted manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Translate a specific message

To translate an individual message in the chat history:

1. Hover over the message card and click the three-dot button.
2. Click **Translate**.
3. Select the target language from the dropdown.

### Input box translation

When **Input Translation** is enabled:

1. In the input box, click the language selector **[1]** to choose the target language (the language the customer understands).
2. Type your message in your language, then click the **Translate** button **[2]** to see the preview.
3. Adjust the translation manually if needed.
4. Click **Send Translation [3]** to send.

<Note>
Your tenant has a translation character quota based on your plan. You can check usage in **Personal settings > Translation**. Additional characters can be purchased as add-ons.
</Note>

## Editing customer details

Click the customer's avatar or the **Expand** button to open the customer details panel:

- **Rename** — click the edit icon next to the name.
- **Reassign** — click the assignment dropdown to change the owner or assigned agent.
- **Add tags** — click "+" in the Tags section.
- **Edit attributes** — click the attribute value, modify it, and click away or press **Enter** to save.
