---
title: "Billing FAQ"
description: "Answers to common questions about YCloud billing, including amount freezing, low balance behaviour, and subscription expiry."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How am I charged for WhatsApp messages?">
    You are charged per **conversation**, not per message. A conversation is a 24-hour window that begins when the first message is sent (either by your business or by the customer).

    The charge for a conversation depends on:
    - The **category** of the conversation (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, or Service).
    - The **country** of the recipient's phone number.

    Within a single 24-hour window, any number of messages can be exchanged at no additional cost. When the window closes, the next message sent opens a new conversation and incurs a new charge.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is amount freezing?">
    When you send a message, YCloud pre-authorises (freezes) an amount from your account balance equal to the estimated cost of the conversation. This ensures sufficient funds are available before the message is delivered.

    Once the delivery outcome is confirmed:
    - If the message is **delivered successfully**, the frozen amount is permanently deducted from your balance.
    - If the message **fails to deliver**, the frozen amount is released and returned to your available balance.

    Frozen amounts are not immediately deducted — they are held while the delivery is in progress.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if my balance is too low to cover a frozen amount?">
    If your available balance is insufficient to cover the frozen amount for a new send, the message will fail and no charge is applied. You need to recharge your balance before attempting to send again.

    To prevent this, configure a [low balance alert](/billing/overview#low-balance-alerts) so you are notified before your balance runs out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why was an amount frozen but not released after a failed message?">
    Frozen amounts are released once YCloud receives the final delivery status from WhatsApp. In some cases, delivery status can take several minutes to arrive. If you see a frozen amount that has not resolved after a reasonable period, check the message status in your message logs. If the issue persists, contact YCloud support.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I get charged for messages that are not delivered?">
    No. If a message fails to deliver (for example, because the recipient's phone is off or the number is invalid), the frozen amount is released and you are not charged for that conversation.

    Note that if the message is delivered but the recipient simply does not read it, the conversation charge still applies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when my subscription expires?">
    When your subscription plan expires, a **15-day buffer period** begins automatically. During this period:
    - Your account remains accessible.
    - You can continue using paid features.
    - You should renew your subscription to avoid interruption.

    After the 15-day buffer period ends, access to premium features is suspended. Your data and contacts are preserved and will be accessible again once you renew.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I set up automatic recharging?">
    YCloud supports automatic top-up for some payment methods. When your balance drops below a threshold, your stored payment method is charged and the balance is topped up automatically. Check **Pricing and Billing > Billing and Recharge** to see if auto-recharge is available for your account and payment method.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I set up a low balance alert?">
    Navigate to **Pricing and Billing > Low Balance Alert**. Set the balance threshold and enter one or more email addresses to notify. When your balance drops below the threshold, an alert email is sent to the configured addresses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see my billing history?">
    Navigate to **Pricing and Billing > Billing and Recharge** to view your recharge history, balance transactions, and invoice records.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are service conversations (customer-initiated) charged differently?">
    Yes. Service conversations — opened when a customer sends a message to your business first — are billed at a different (typically lower) rate than business-initiated Marketing, Utility, or Authentication conversations. Pricing varies by country. See the rate card in your YCloud account for exact figures.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currencies does YCloud support?">
    YCloud supports billing in USD. Contact your account manager or YCloud support if you have questions about currency or payment methods available in your region.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
